# Race Refinery coach phrase registry: every clip a voice pack can provide.
#
# `[tier category]` opens a section; each line below it is `key=prompt`, where the
# prompt is what the speaker says into the mic. Tiers:
#   spotter  - stand-alone radio lines (flags, traffic, race clock, fuel stock calls)
#   engineer - numbers and glue words chained into lap times, gaps, deltas, fuel counts
#
# Numbers are recorded as 0-20 plus the round tens; 21-99 are spoken as tens + ones
# (29 = n20 + n9) and 100+ digit by digit. Keys are part of the pack format: renaming
# one orphans that clip in every existing pack.
